BRAVO requires registration and fees (BL1–BL3); IRIS supports paid ad-hoc access (BL4). No IRIS per-document price was verified in the retained sources. The assistant should track requests and ingest user-provided official copies; it should not silently purchase records, operate private accounts, or turn non-retrieval into a legal-status conclusion.
